markjin is a lightweight research-signal workflow: collect → digest → produce → publish → feedback → improve.

Phase-one sources: arXiv, FRED, and X. Every claim in the daily Chinese digest comes with an evidence link and does not go beyond the content of the original source. Raw data is archived byte-for-byte in [markjin-data](https://github.com/markjin-lab/markjin-data).
