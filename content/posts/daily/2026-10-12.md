**1. 甜甜圈上的伊辛模型：从统计力学看拓扑量子纠错的各个区间（Ising on the Donut: Regimes of Topological Quantum Error Correction from Statistical Mechanics）**

> An exact mapping to the 2D Ising model produces closed-form logical failure rates for the toric code, offering an analytic tool kit for benchmarking topological codes beyond current computational limits.
>
> 译文：通过精确映射到二维伊辛模型，得到了环面码逻辑失效率的闭式表达，为超出当前计算极限的拓扑码基准测试提供了一套解析工具。

一手来源：PRX Quantum 7, 043004，2026-10-06 发表 [1]。

**2. 对偶极致热效应具有韧性的空穴自旋（Hole Spin Resilient to Dipole-Induced Thermal Effects）**

> A silicon hole spin unveils an electric origin of thermal frequency shifts and exhibits a magnetic-field sweet-spot immune to those, guiding the design of thermally robust semiconductor qubits.
>
> 译文：一个硅空穴自旋揭示了热致频移的电学起源，并呈现出一个不受此类频移影响的磁场“甜点”，为设计热稳健的半导体量子比特提供指导。

一手来源：PRX Quantum 7, 043005，2026-10-07 发表 [2]。

**3. NASA 征集美国企业的商业空间站计划**

> On Friday, the agency released its final Request for Proposals inviting industry to submit plans for the next generation of commercial space stations.
>
> 译文：周五，该机构发布了最终版征求建议书，邀请企业提交下一代商业空间站的计划。

**4. NASA 演示下一代隔热罩技术**

> NASA is testing heat shields that can keep astronauts safe as they return from missions to the Moon and Mars in an unusual way: by taking out the trash.
>
> 译文：NASA 正在以一种不寻常的方式测试能在宇航员从月球和火星任务返回时保障其安全的隔热罩：把垃圾带出去。

> The experiment, called the Kentucky Reentry Probe Experiment (KREPE-3), is the third in a series of low-cost, high-impact missions that use the final moments of the cargo spacecraft’s lifespan to gather valuable data.
>
> 译文：这项名为肯塔基再入探测器实验（KREPE-3）的实验，是一系列低成本、高影响任务中的第三次，这些任务利用货运飞船寿命的最后时刻收集有价值的数据。

一手来源：NASA 艾姆斯研究中心新闻，2026-10-09 [4]。
