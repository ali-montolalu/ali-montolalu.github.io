## Goal
Cognitive practice and self-evolution of the Unified Systems View, augmented by AI assistance.

## Evidence-first principles
1. Every claim must come with an evidence link (the original source).
2. Write nothing beyond the source; inferences must be clearly labelled.
3. Raw data is archived byte for byte with its sha256 recorded, and never modified.
4. Daily review starts at 06:30 and posts are published at 07:00 (SGT); improvement proposals are reviewed every Monday at 10:00.
5. All feedback and improvements are tracked through public Issues.
